## 1. GR3-01 · closed

**The fix.** `eebf7b9`: for `uniq` and `xxd`, `operandCount` ([RiskPolicy.swift:802](../../Sources/Engine/Auto/RiskPolicy.swift#L802))
counts a bare `-` and every word after `--` as an operand, and `writingReadOnlyUse`
([:783](../../Sources/Engine/Auto/RiskPolicy.swift#L783)) sends two or more to the reviewer. `013a161` adds what vim's
`xxd` does: it stops reading options at its first operand (the argument loop in [xxd.c](https://github.com/vim/vim/blob/master/src/xxd/xxd.c)
ends at the first word that is not an option, after `--`, or at a bare `-`), so every later word counts too
([:793](../../Sources/Engine/Auto/RiskPolicy.swift#L793)). I had found that second case in the source myself before I
saw the commit.

**Spellings.** The decisions come from the tip's `RiskPolicy.swift`, `ShellAnalysis.swift` and `OptionSyntax.swift`.

## 5. Known limitations

- `uniq IN -NAME` and `uniq - --` are allowed: BSD `uniq` and GNU `uniq` under `POSIXLY_CORRECT=1` stop reading options
  at their first operand and write a file with a leading dash in the working folder. GNU by default and busybox refuse
  them, BelloCode runs commands in Linux containers and only `docker` on the Mac, and no policy file has such a name.
  To close it, count operands after the first for `uniq` as for `xxd`.
- The policy version is still `bellocode-auto-5` after four changes to its rules (`84b6543`, `95268f0`, `eebf7b9`,
  `013a161`).
- Round 3's limitations stand unchanged: a program named by its path is judged by its basename; rg takes `--pre` from
  `RIPGREP_CONFIG_PATH`; a committed secret is readable through an object name.
- Astra's two R6 limitations stand as recorded: a failed first send's restore is dropped after ten minutes if the new
  thread's draft never loads, and a follow-up typed just before a new thread is created can miss its save.
